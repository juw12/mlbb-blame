Paste box, format and tips

Fastest: open your end-of-match screenshot, long-press to copy the text (iPhone Live Text or Google Lens) and paste it here. Or let the app read the screenshot itself (after the first read it also works offline). One player per line works best: name, hero, K/D/A, gold, hero dmg, dmg taken, turret dmg. You check every number before analyzing.

Skip a player by leaving the name empty. Damage accepts 84512, 84,512 or 84.5k. Enter / Next on the keypad jumps to the next box. In a hurry? Leave turret damage (or any gold or damage column) empty for everyone and it is left out of the score. Your entry is saved on this device as you type, and a name you have analyzed before fills in its hero and role.

Squad history

Scores compare each player's share of team gold, hero damage, damage taken, turret damage, kill participation, deaths and KDA against what their role normally contributes. Role baselines are this app's own estimates, not official Moonton data, and end-screen stats can't see rotations, vision, objective calls or the draft — use it as evidence, not a court ruling.
