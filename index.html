📷 Fill from a screenshot or pasted text

Fastest: open your end-of-match screenshot, long-press to copy the text (iPhone Live Text or Google Lens) and paste it here. Or let the app read the screenshot itself. One player per line works best: name, hero, K/D/A, gold, hero dmg, dmg taken, turret dmg. You check every number before analyzing.

Skip a player by leaving the name empty. Damage accepts 84512, 84,512 or 84.5k. Your entry is saved on this device as you type.
